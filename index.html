What is this?

This tool shows empty classrooms at your college right now — find a free room to study, do assignments, or sit with your group.

Slots that have already passed are hidden automatically. Only rooms empty from the current time onwards are shown.

Switch days using the tabs. Filter by building using the chips. Use the edit icon next to the time to browse any hour. Use Find rooms free between two times to see rooms that stay free the whole time (e.g. 10 AM – 12 PM).

Each room shows how long it stays free, longest first. Breaks and lunch count as free.
